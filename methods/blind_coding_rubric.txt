# Independent source coding instructions

This is a neutral documentary content-analysis task. Read the complete relevant primary submission, including annexes, or the actor's complete contribution within a compiled hearing. Do not read prior coded datasets, findings, earlier reviews, article drafts, or files outside the assigned packet and its linked primary/derived sources. Do not infer the prior coder's answer. Do not treat document length or repeated wording as additional observations.

The packet deliberately omits stance, rationales, selected supporting passages and selection reasons. Actor/source identity is supplied to locate the contribution. Verify source dates, identity and legal status from the document rather than accepting metadata automatically. For a large compiled volume, the actor's complete testimony, questions/answers and adopted attachments are the relevant broad context; identify precisely what you read. Where a contribution cannot be recovered or read adequately, mark it unverified rather than no position.

## Mechanisms

A: the issuer pays holder interest or yield. B: reserve/backing-asset income is distributed to holders. C: a third party/intermediary remunerates holders. D: an activity-based reward/rebate/discount independent of holding duration. E: the issuer earns reserve/backing-asset income, including central-bank balances.

A does not establish B. E does not establish B. A question about the use of reserve income without identifying holders is not B. A money-market fund purchased using a stablecoin is a different product. Contingent indemnity of lost principal is not ordinarily passive income remuneration; preserve explicit boundary cases. A B-funded transaction rebate may be coded B and D only with the activity qualification made explicit; do not turn it into passive B permission.

For each mechanism assign permit, permit conditionally, prohibit, no position, ambiguous, or not_addressed. The last is a coverage value: no relevant category discussion. No position means the category is actually described, questioned or contemplated but no normative recommendation is made. Silence receives not_addressed at category level and no position overall. Ambiguous requires relevant text whose normative direction is unresolved, or conflicting explicit positions. Description of an existing law/model is no position unless the actor adopts or recommends it. The issuing authority's own legal rule is a policy position.

A substantive condition must impose an actual safeguard or policy limitation beyond the definition of the category. Defining D as non-duration/activity-linked is not itself a conditional-permission safeguard. Preserve issuer independence, funding restrictions, caps or other additional conditions.

**B evidence discipline:** assign a substantive B position only when reserve/backing income and payment to holders are explicit in the actor's argument or rule. Broad A/C bans may legally encompass B, but record that separately in `category_B_scope_inference` with its text and limitation; do not count an inferred B prohibition as an explicit B policy argument. For other categories also distinguish express text from scope interpretation in `category_evidence_basis`.

Overall position aggregates A–D only. All unaddressed/no-position categories -> no position. One substantive stance -> that stance. Permit plus permit conditionally -> permit conditionally. Other conflicting directions -> ambiguous. E-only normative positions do not supply an overall holder stance. If the payer is unspecified, preserve `unspecified_holder_position` and use it in aggregation without inventing A/C/B.

## Rationales

Record yes only with a passage linking the rationale to remuneration or its directly relevant proposed design; a general paragraph elsewhere on stablecoin risk is not enough. Record no for absence of invocation, not disagreement. Supply quotation, pinpoint and the direction of the argument.

- deposit_displacement_bank_funding: deposit migration/substitution, funding, lending or credit.
- monetary_policy_transmission: policy effectiveness or transmission, specified in context.
- financial_stability_run_risk: runs, contagion, liquidity or systemic instability.
- payments_vs_investment: payments distinguished from saving, investment or store of value.
- legal_classification: legal deposit/e-money/security/CIS/AIF or equivalent classification; economic analogy with bank deposits alone is insufficient.
- competition_innovation_consumer_welfare: competition, innovation, access, choice, consumer costs/benefits.
- commercial_viability: issuer/intermediary operating costs, revenues or ability to operate.

## Reserve safety versus income allocation

Recode this variable independently for every assigned observation. Use the following subsidiary classification and record the exact passage:

- `explicit_analytical_distinction`: expressly distinguishes principal backing/redemption safety or liquidity from entitlement/allocation of the backing income, or analyzes their compatibility as separate design choices. A source may make the distinction and still oppose remuneration because it changes run incentives; distinction is not agreement that remuneration is riskless.
- `joint_mention_only`: mentions full backing, reserves or redemption and holder remuneration without expressly analyzing their relationship or separately allocating principal/income. Mentioning E beside A is insufficient.
- `explicit_equation`: affirmatively equates or denies the separability of backing protection and income allocation. Quote the exact argument; do not infer this from a ban.
- `not_addressed`: neither relationship is analyzed.
- `uncertain`: insufficient context to choose; explain.

Map to the legacy flag: explicit_analytical_distinction -> yes; explicit_equation -> no; joint_mention_only/not_addressed -> not_addressed; uncertain -> ambiguous. A legal rule reserving backing income to issuer while restricting holder interest can be a separation of entitlements, but does not automatically analyze the safety/distribution relationship. Explain which proposition the text actually makes.

## Negative review

Read full relevant prose, headings, footnotes, tables and annexes, including indirect economic descriptions and appropriate translated terms. Keyword searches aid navigation but cannot certify semantic absence. Inspect image/scanned pages when extraction is incomplete. Mark `review_depth` as full_document_semantic, full_actor_contribution_semantic, partial_context_only, or unavailable. Record page/section coverage, missing material and any visually checked OCR pages. Never call a partial/blocked read a full negative.

## Output per observation

Return JSON with observation_id; reviewer_id; position; categories A–E; unspecified_holder_position if needed; category_evidence_basis; category_B_scope_inference; all seven rationale flags; reserve_safety_distribution_distinguished; reserve_income_separation_level; source_metadata_verified (source_id, date, actor, title/version, legal_status and pinpoint); review_depth; reviewed_sources; coverage; quotations/pinpoints supporting each positive code; negative_review_note if appropriate; ambiguities; source_access_failures. Keep quotations short and attributable; paraphrases must be labelled. Report contrary evidence within the source. Do not infer private influence, motive or a preferred thesis.
