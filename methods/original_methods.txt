# Methods and replication — version 2

The research combines structured comparative content analysis with bounded process tracing. Research cutoff is **2026-10-02**; recovery and verification continued through **2026-10-04**. Retrieval after the cutoff does not make post-cutoff policy material eligible. No capture hypothesis supplies a coding rule. The archive is a documentary research edition; availability-selected counts cannot estimate stakeholder preferences or lobbying influence.

## Frame, discovery and boundaries

The source universe precedes analysis and retains official policy episodes, document versions, individual inputs, attached/adopted submissions, summaries, exclusions, duplicate routes and unavailable leads. Discovery uses official indexes, legislative/version chains, agency comment metadata/pagination, linked attachments, archived official repositories, then respondent-hosted primary copies. Search-engine hits are leads only. No source position is substituted from secondary reporting. Source register rows are not independent respondents.

Four discovery strata remain separate: complete enumerated published collections; substantially enumerated named/index collections; targeted historical searches; opportunistic primary recoveries. A complete metadata frame is not a full-text census. `corpus_coverage.csv` supplies episode-level enumeration, recovery and inspection dimensions. Public submission totals can count campaigns or records differently from visible docket documents; neither is automatically a unique-actor denominator.

EU searching covers e-money ancestry from 1998, the 2005 review and 2006 report, 2008–09 institutional texts, pre-MiCA 2017–19 material, the 2020 consultation/impact assessment/version chain, 2022 ECON/Council texts, adopted MiCA and 2026 review inputs. English, French, German, Italian, Spanish and Dutch terminology is logged in jurisdiction search records. The published MiCA collection has 197 questionnaire contributions and 64 attachments; the API also exposes a rejected Euroclear record, explaining the observed 197/198 publication discrepancy without proving the Commission’s internal counting practice. Some published cells are fragments. The digital-finance strategy collection has 186 spreadsheet responses;19 potentially relevant contributions and the complete ROFIEG report received subsequent semantic review. Unavailable attachments remain gaps.

UK searching covers FSA CP117 and PS117, the 2010–11 e-money implementation, Treasury policy development, Bank 2021/22,2023 and 2025/26 material, FCA proposals/final rules and parliamentary evidence. All 29 named Bank 2025 organisational leads were pursued; named leads cannot identify the anonymous 2022 pass-through proponents. The Bank’s 86 responses/73 organisations are separate from recovered public documents. Failed Lords routes were revisited using official readers and primary copies; parsed readers are preserved as derivatives, not original PDF bytes.

US channels distinguish congressional testimony, written hearing answers, committee submissions, amendments/votes/reports, public letters and agency notice-and-comment. The register covers major federal bills and implementation through the cutoff. Published OCC/Treasury/NCUA comment frames were enumerated, but full-text coverage remains uneven. In the NCUA prudential episode, all 30 PDF attachments and the DOCX alternative were recovered; 26 newly screened attachment records consolidated into 22 additional actor–episode contributions, all receiving fresh blind review. Four of those contributions remain partial because of missing incorporated texts. Other docket populations remain incompletely recovered or inspected. The FDIC published index’s 66 links include supplements and meetings; they are not 66 unique respondents. Late agency proposals are not treated as effective law.

Broad search families include interest/yield/remuneration/return/reward/benefit/income; reserve or backing income; interest-bearing, savings/store-of-value/investment; deposit substitution/flight, bank funding/credit/lending; monetary policy/transmission; and close translated forms. Countervailing searches include bank support, issuer opposition, consumer objections, earlier public-interest rationales, risky-yield incentives, deposit/e-money boundaries and narrower alternatives. Query and recovery logs remain inspectable; incompletely retained historical query metadata is not represented as exhaustive.

## Observation, coding and evidence

The quantitative unit is one actor–episode. Repeated text and longer submissions receive no extra weight. Supplements and adopted documents enter the same actor–episode, preserving original authors and attribution chains. Joint coalitions remain joint observations; institutional-policy observations, named public-authority inputs, private inputs, anonymous individuals and anonymous aggregates are separated. Exact repeated passages are consolidated while raw coding rows survive.

A–E are defined in codebook.md. CategoryB requires an explicit reserve-income/holder-allocation connection; broad legal coverage inferred from A/C is a separate annotation. E is never evidence of B permission. Overall holder stance aggregates A–D and any supported unspecified-payer proposition, excluding E. Normative recommendations are distinguished from description and hypothetical models. Reserve safety is analytically distinguished from holder allocation only when the passage expressly treats those as separate design questions; joint mention is insufficient.

Full semantic review means complete document or complete relevant actor contribution, including footnotes/annexes where recovered. A legal-provision check does not certify every page of a large act. Inaccessible content, truncated questionnaire cells, missing incorporation and respondent summaries cannot establish whole-submission absence. Null fields mean unresolved source coverage; visible positive evidence can be retained while unrelated absences remain null. Descriptive no-position and not-addressed are distinct from missingness. Machine-only negatives are retained for discovery but excluded from strong historical-origin inference.

## Independent review and adjudication

The original first pass was frozen before fresh reviewers received neutral source packets. The mandatory sample contains all originally substantive A–E, explicitB, central/priority and institutional-policy observations plus a new deterministic ceil 25% sample of remaining negatives stratified by jurisdiction×observation kind×actor type. Late mandatory inputs receive supplementary frozen batches. All 22 newly coded NCUA contributions were independently recoded, including their negative findings, in addition to the original stratified negative sample. Reviewers were procedurally blinded to first codes, rationale flags, findings, desired thesis and selection reasons. Separate AI contexts, not filesystem security isolation, supplied blinding. This is not external human legal validation.

The instrument was clarified before/during review to distinguish A–E, explicitB, missingness, description, and analytical separation. Agreement therefore compares frozen first coding with a refined instrument; it is not a pure unchanged-codebook inter-rater experiment. Cohen’sκ and exact agreement are computed before adjudication, fieldwise on non-null pairs; returned review, classifiable stance and complete-context coverage are separate. No post-adjudication agreement is presented as independent reliability. Every disagreement retains first/second/final codes, primary pinpoint, evidence and reason. Administrative null decisions rely on coverage records, not fabricated quotations.

Source-specific reviewed passages replace selected-context first-pass argument rows for reviewed observations. Original rows remain inactive in argument_coding_rows.csv. Adoption requires explicit incorporation/alignment and identifiable version; a later published document is not automatically backdated to an earlier adoption. Conflicting adopted positions remain ambiguous. Original passage dates remain distinct from hearing/episode dates; undated written answers do not inherit oral-hearing dates.

## Quantitative and process-tracing analysis

Tables report actor–episode counts with eligible/classifiable denominators, unknown coverage and population kind. Repeat actors across episodes count repeatedly. Unique-actor sensitivity uses explicit aliases only, leaves coalitions intact and reports nonexclusive ever-observed positions plus latest dated input. Rationale flags are nonexclusive and counted once per actor–episode; E-only economic arguments retain their category context. Sensitivities separately remove partially recovered episodes, anonymous records, screen-only negatives and post-enactment review/implementation; the joint exclusion is also reported. A separately named conservative check removes all negative/unknown observations without complete blind context, including partial reviews; it is not labelled machine-only. Strict machine-only exclusion requires an explicit machine-only review status and does not relabel manual selected-context review as machine-only. Sparse or empty subsets do not establish robustness. No significance tests, population estimates or causal regressions are used.

Chronology.csv is regenerated from audited retained claim units. Older event coding is preserved in raw jurisdiction handoffs but is not silently carried into the current chronology. Mechanical earliest-position/rationale minima are separately regenerated from eligible dated passages, with searched universe and overturning gaps. All priority classifications remain low where earlier public input is unavailable. They are discovery aids, not article-level claims of origin. Official proposal, permission model, private advocacy, enactment, publication and commencement are distinct events. Correspondence, sequence and textual similarity establish alignment only; direct evidence is required for influence and separately for capture.

## Preservation and reproduction

Original v 1 files and ZIP are preserved and checked against the initial preservation manifest. Original URLs, recorded final URLs, dates, MIME/status, byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes are retained in source_provenance.csv and recovery receipts. Missing redirect/MIME/date metadata stays missing. archive_manifest.csv identifies exact byte objects; parsed reader, OCR, extraction and visual screenshots are derivatives. Exact copies retain every provenance route. Decisive scanned/OCR passages have visual-check receipts; no derivative hash is represented as an original-source hash.

Run `python scripts/rebuild.py` inside the version 2 folder with Python 3.12 or later and the dependencies in requirements.txt. The tested environment is recorded in runtime_versions.json and requirements-lock.txt. Byte identity is tested within that environment, not asserted across operating systems or plotting-library versions. Rebuilding uses only frozen local inputs and explicit patches, not live network retrieval. Two clean runs are compared across generated data, narrative and figure outputs in reproduction_check.json. Release packaging separately verifies every ZIP member against release_manifest.csv; archive SHA-256 is outside the ZIP to avoid self-reference. Technical reproducibility does not certify documentary completeness or legal correctness.
