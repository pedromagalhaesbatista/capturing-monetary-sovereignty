CODING INSTRUMENT AND PROPAGATION — PACKAGE OF 10 OCTOBER 2026

The substantive evidence cutoff is 2 October 2026. That is not a claim that all
coding was completed that day. The archived clarification is dated 3 October;
model-assisted review and adjudication continued after the evidence cutoff.
The separate qualitative retrieval/inspection supplement is dated 9 October.

The original protocol, final codebook, neutral blind rubric and Clarification 001
are preserved here. Changes separated issuer interest (A), express reserve-income
pass-through (B), intermediary rewards (C), activity benefits (D), and issuer
reserve earnings (E); distinguished descriptive no-position from not-addressed;
kept broad-ban coverage of B separate from an express B argument; and narrowed
the safety/income distinction to holder allocation, preserving E-only analysis
separately. Commercial viability was clarified to include banks, issuers and
intermediaries where operating revenue/cost or ability to operate was argued.

Second-session reviews cover 320 of 517 observations; 197 have no such second-review
record. Absence of a second review does not establish that no first-stage semantic
reading occurred. The sample design targeted substantive/central/institutional
records and a stratified subset of negatives, with later mandatory batches.
The package does not certify that every observation potentially affected by every
definition change was reread under the final instrument. That remains a limitation.

For all 320 reviewed observations, reconcile_reviews.py reconstructs 15 final
fields from second codes plus the recorded adjudication override, then compares
them with the final actor dataset: 4,800 comparisons. The original field-level audit is retained in audit/field_reconciliation.csv.
The recorded dataset matches all 4,800. There are 192 observation-level adjudication decisions. This mechanical
propagation check is distinct from validating those semantic decisions against
the original contributions. The remaining 197 records are listed separately.

Figure 1 and stance totals are recalculated from the final actor rows, never from
summed quotations. Category/position labels on V2ADJ passage rows describe a
decision context and can differ from final category-specific codes. The apparent
cross-products are listed in passage_cross_product_diagnostics.csv. Final actor
category fields govern counts; those rows are not independent votes. The archived
wording is retained, not silently recoded to force agreement.

Among 245 private no-position rows, 108 have a verified complete-context second
review, 5 a qualified second review, and 132 no second-review record. Some of the 132
have first-stage semantic review; others retain keyword/context or survey-answer
screening. Inspect their recorded scopes individually. All 22 private unknown
rows have partial-context second review and insufficient-to-classify status.
No position means no overall recommendation was recorded in the inspected
material; it does not uniformly certify whole-contribution silence. The strict
negative-coverage sensitivity output is an exclusion exercise, not a recoding.

Agreement statistics compare earlier codes with a refined instrument. They are
not reliability under an unchanged instrument, and not independent human coding.
Safety/income distinction frequency is unsuitable as a robust comparative result.
