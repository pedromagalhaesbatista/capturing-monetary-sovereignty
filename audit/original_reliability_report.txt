# Fresh blind coding audit

320 actor–episode reviews returned (289 overall stances classifiable) from 320 selected observations; 244 have verified complete-document or complete-actor context. The remaining returned codes retain partial-source qualifications. Population: 517 observations.

This is independent AI-agent consistency checking, not external legal or human validation. Fresh reviewers received neutral source packets without first-pass stances, rationales, findings or selection reasons. Separate model contexts provided procedural blinding; shared filesystem permissions did not provide technical access isolation. Packet/source hashes and frozen first codes are retained.

The instrument was refined to separate A–E, descriptive no-position from not-addressed, explicit B from scope inference, and analytic safety/distribution separation from joint mention. Clarification001 was sent to all blind reviewers before first-code disclosure. Agreement below therefore measures an old/frozen-code versus revised-instrument audit, not pure inter-rater reliability under an unchanged codebook. It is computed before adjudication; adjudicated agreement is not presented as independent reliability.

Full jurisdiction statistics and confusion matrices: [statistics](v2/reliability_statistics.csv), [confusion matrices](v2/reliability_confusion.csv). Mandatory-set and new stratified-negative coverage: [coverage](v2/independent_review_coverage.csv).

Null fields represent unknown source coverage, not silence or a substantive ambiguous stance; paired agreement excludes null fields and separately reports classifiable coverage. Partial reviews are sufficient only for the expressly verified positive passages or identified legal provisions; they cannot certify submission-wide silence. Published EU CSV fragments, incomplete reader extracts and incorporated but unread attachments are counted as partial. Every unresolved item remains in the coverage file.

The sample contains every originally substantive/category-B/institutional/central-or-priority observation plus a deterministic ceil25% stratified sample of remaining negatives by jurisdiction × observation kind × actor type; late central and recovered inputs are supplementary frozen batches. Newly substantive codes discovered by recoding are retained and adjudicated.

Adjudications retain first, second and final codes with source pinpoints and reasons in [adjudication log](adjudication_log.md).

The safety/distribution-separation variable warrants special caution: exact agreement is 87.0% but Cohen’s κ is 0.298 across 316 classifiable pairs. This is not evidence that the earlier reliability concern has disappeared. The refined distinction and imbalanced category frequencies affect the comparison; inspect the confusion matrix and every positive primary passage. Do not treat frequencies of explicit analytical separation as a robust comparative finding.
